Who we are

Angle Builders Limited is a family run business started by Daniel Gallagher in 1988. Over the years we have built a team of fully qualified, experienced and insured trades and project managers who work directly for us.

Our aim has been the same from the start: to take homes that no longer fit the way families live and turn them into modern spaces to work, eat, play and relax, with as little upheaval to you as possible.
